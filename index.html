Offline data & backup tips

Local Storage: Your tasks, start dates, and streak history are stored strictly on this device and browser. No data is sent to external servers or cloud databases.

When to Export: Export a backup regularly, especially before clearing browser cookies/site data, resetting browser settings, or changing devices.

Prevent Data Loss: Uninstalling the PWA or clearing browser site data will remove local storage. Keep your exported JSON backup in a safe folder.

Restoring: Click Import Backup anytime to restore your tasks and complete history from a previously exported backup file.
